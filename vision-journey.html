✦ OUR VISION & JOURNEY

A vision born to bring the Bible to life.

Rooted in faith, Christian upbringing, service and a desire to communicate the Gospel in ways people can understand and enjoy.

DECEMBER 18, 2004

THE VISION BEGINS

The vision was birthed on December 18, 2004. What began as a God-inspired desire to communicate His Word would gradually develop into a broader mission of Bible teaching, storytelling and digital Christian outreach.

CHRISTIAN FOUNDATION

FAITH & SERVICE

Pastor Abayomi was raised in a Christian family where faith and service to God formed an important part of his upbringing. His parents have faithfully served as ministers of the Gospel for many years.

JULY 2005

CHILDREN'S MINISTRY & THE FIRST BOOK

Pastor Abayomi began his ministry in the Redeemed Christian Church of God as a children's teacher. His passion for sharing the Gospel inspired him to write his first book in July 2005.

GROWING YEARS

SPIRITUAL GROWTH & MINISTRY

Over time, Pastor Abayomi continued to grow spiritually in the Lord. One of the ministries that emerged from this journey is PASTOR ABAYOMI BIBLE STORIES.

TODAY

A GROWING DIGITAL MISSION

The vision continues through Bible stories, digital platforms, Bible applications, devotionals, prayer resources, games, Christian education, videos and digital media.

Our Mission

To reach the world globally for God through the Gospel.

PASTOR ABAYOMI BIBLE STORIES seeks to use traditional and modern digital channels to make biblical truth understandable, engaging and accessible across generations.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family

Pastor Abayomi is married to Mrs. Adebosola Oyenuga, and they are blessed with children. Family remains an important part of the wider vision.

🎓

Education & Professional Development

Pastor Abayomi holds a Bachelor's degree from Babcock University, an MBA, and several professional certifications.

The Journey Continues

The vision is growing into a Christian digital ecosystem where people can read, discover, pray, learn, play and grow in faith.
